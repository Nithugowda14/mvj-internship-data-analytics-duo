# Project 2: Customer Retention Plan

## Goal
Encourage more first-time customers to place a second order.

## What the analysis shows
In this dataset, 591 of 31,696 customers placed more than one order, a repeat-customer rate of 1.86%.
The RFM analysis also identified one-time customers and repeat customers who may be at risk of becoming
inactive. These are historical results from a practice dataset.

## Proposed small test
1. Select a group of one-time customers who can be contacted.
2. Split them into two similar groups:
   - **Test group:** receives a helpful follow-up message with relevant products or a small offer.
   - **Comparison group:** receives no campaign during the test.
3. Run the test for a set period and record the results.

## Measures to compare
- Percentage of customers who place another order
- Number of repeat orders
- Revenue per customer
- Refunds and campaign costs
- Profit, if complete cost data is available

## Decision
Continue or expand the campaign only if the test group improves repeat purchasing and the financial result 
remains positive compared with the comparison group.

## Limitations
The dataset is historical and does not prove that a campaign will work today. The 1.86% figure describes repeat
purchasing across the available data period. Order value is not profit, and results should be checked using the
full cohort analysis before making broader decisions.
