Customer retention
├── How many customers return?
│   ├── Repeat customers = customers with more than one order
│   └── Repeat-customer rate = repeat customers ÷ total customers
├── When do customers return?
│   └── Cohort retention = customers ordering in a later month
│       ÷ customers in their first-order-month cohort
├── Which customers should we focus on?
│   └── RFM segmentation
│       ├── Recency = days since the customer's last order
│       ├── Frequency = number of orders
│       └── Monetary = total order value
└── How do we track sales alongside retention?
    ├── Orders = distinct order IDs
    ├── Sales revenue = total order value
    └── Average order value = sales revenue ÷ orders


Results from this analysis

- Customers: 31,696
- Repeat customers: 591
- Repeat-customer rate: 1.86%
- Weighted Month 1 cohort retention: 0.96%
The repeat-customer rate measures customers who ordered more than once over the full dataset.
 Month 1 cohort retention measures customers who ordered again in the calendar month after
 their first order. Sales revenue and monetary value are before refunds, so they are not profit figures.
