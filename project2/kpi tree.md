# Project 2 KPI Tree: Customer Retention

## Main business question

How can the business encourage more customers to place repeat orders?

## KPI tree

```text
Customer retention
├── Do customers come back?
│   ├── Repeat customers = customers with more than one order
│   └── Repeat-customer rate = repeat customers ÷ all customers
├── When do customers return?
│   ├── Group customers by the month of their first order
│   └── Month N retention = cohort customers who order in month N ÷ original cohort size
├── Who should the business focus on?
│   ├── Recency = days since the customer's last order
│   ├── Frequency = number of orders
│   └── Monetary value = total order value
└── What value do orders bring?
    ├── Orders = count of distinct order IDs
    ├── Sales revenue = sum of order value
    └── Average order value (AOV) = sales revenue ÷ orders
